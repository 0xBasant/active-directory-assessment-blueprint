# Authorization and Responsible-Use Notice

This repository is for defensive security planning and assessments performed with explicit written authorization.

The engagement owner must document:

- the legal entity granting authorization;
- the exact networks, domains, forests, tenants, hosts, identities, applications, and data in scope;
- permitted techniques and prohibited actions;
- the approved time window and source systems;
- emergency contacts, stop conditions, and incident-handling procedures;
- data retention, evidence transfer, and destruction requirements.

Technique names such as DCSync, Kerberoasting, credential dumping, authentication relay, and forged Kerberos tickets describe test objectives. Their inclusion is not permission to run them. High-impact techniques require separate opt-in approval and human authorization at execution time.

Nothing in this repository is legal advice, a guarantee of security, or a substitute for qualified operators. If scope or ownership is uncertain, do not test.
