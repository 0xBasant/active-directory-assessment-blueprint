# Contributing

Contributions should improve authorized assessment planning, safety controls, reporting, or defensive validation.

Do not commit:

- customer identifiers, credentials, hashes, tickets, memory dumps, or raw assessment evidence;
- live exploit code, credential-theft automation, persistence mechanisms, or payloads;
- instructions that bypass authorization or safety gates;
- vendor claims presented as independently verified facts.

For research additions, include a primary source where possible, the access date, and one of the evidence labels defined in the README. Changes to safety gates should explain the risk tradeoff.
